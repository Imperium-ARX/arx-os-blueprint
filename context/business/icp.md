# ICP - the deep layer

## Layer 1 - Segmentation

The distinct buyer clusters, if more than one. For each: who they are, what they run, which offer fits. Filled during onboarding discovery. When the owner states a segment shift directly, record it as a dated section tagged `[OWNER EXPLICITLY SAID]`.

## Layer 2 - Headspace

What the buyer believes, fears, and wants; their awareness stage (a distribution, not a point); their sophistication about solutions like ours. Filled during onboarding discovery, refined from transcripts.

## Layer 3 - Verbatim language (harvest, don't invent)

The exact words buyers use, harvested from real sources only - transcripts, emails, reviews. Every quote cites its source file. Quotes without a source are `[UNVERIFIED]` and never make it into content. Subsections as this grows:

- Objections and limiting beliefs (ranked)
- Questions they ask
- Pain points, in their words
- Buying signals

## Disqualifiers

Hard rules for who we do NOT sell to, from real deal history. Filled as deals close or die.

## The fit test

3-5 yes/no questions that qualify a prospect against the layers above. Filled once the pattern is visible.
